## Operator priority refinement

Immediate streaming and speech overlap take priority over exhaustive rubric
coverage. Missing explanatory detail does not justify delaying a response for
a second inference/rewrite pass. Wrong calculations, unsupported house/timing
claims and fabricated user biography remain substantive issues. Fictional
character episodes are welcomed, but need persistent specific canon to prevent
contradiction. Actual model training records are not available through the chat
API; a provider-reasoning experiment must be distinguished from fictional lore.

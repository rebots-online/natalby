# Continuous voice and cutout correction journey

This is an extension of the opening/atelier journey, covering VC01–VC08. Record
one uninterrupted visit to the deployed reading room without erasing the existing
conversation. Preserve the generated words, routed model and completion metadata
as evidence; do not retain credentials in the recording or repository.

1. Reload the delivered app and bring the reading room into focus. Show Natally
   idle, blinking and tilting over the original sky, then the scrying head motion.
   Keep the antenna and both turban edges visible at a useful scale: no rectangular
   room patch should travel with the head and no edge of the turban should vanish.
2. Ask an ordinary chart question without prompting for slang or style. Hear the
   entire waiting sentence, including "look", with matching mouth activity; watch
   her scry while the answer is prepared. Capture the versioned waiting asset used.
3. Read and hear the fresh response from its opening through its digressions and
   return to the subject. Demonstrate her own Gen-Z diction and associative imagery,
   not a formal personality verdict with slang appended. Chart positions/aspects
   stay factual; imagined meanings remain images or possibilities. Show real focus
   cues and relationship lines while switching between main and inset chart.
4. Continue into Insta-now: generation is one click, not a tiny reading. Observe a
   substantial current-sky exploration with actual observation facts and the same
   personality, without a word-count instruction leaking into the conversation.
   Let the wait/narration/idle transitions also expose the animated head outline.
5. Replay the new response, then leave the reading room visible with a screenshot
   and the continuous recording. Record which scenes actually ran and any remaining
   limitation. Existing historical responses are not rewritten as new output.

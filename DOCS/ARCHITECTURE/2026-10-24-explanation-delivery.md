# English explanations and immediate spoken delivery

This increment extends LC01, ST01–05, DR03, STYLE-1/2 and ROOM-1. Existing
whole-product, billing, archive, device and listening obligations remain open.

## Requirements and checklist mapping

- ED01 / ED-A: Plain English introduces each relation with its angular meaning,
  interpretive role and brief historical rationale, then explains both endpoints.
  Distinguish a moving current planet from the fixed birth-time position, explain
  why the natal endpoint personalizes this comparison, and use everyday examples.
  Explain new aspect types once, then remind briefly. Geometry is calculated;
  symbolic tradition is interpretation. Never invent an aspect or its origin.
  Preserve Natally's casual independent voice. Operator refinement, 24 October:
  remove the Merlin alternative and the reading-depth onboarding step. One
  professional educational standard applies to every new reading, including
  legacy profiles that previously selected Merlin. Historical wording stays.
  READING_RUBRIC.md is the shared executable prompt rubric for planets/points,
  signs, houses, angles, aspects, current-to-natal and partner comparisons,
  motion/timing and overall synthesis. Explanation is adapted to each theme;
  an unexplained label plus metaphor fails the rubric.
- ED02 / ED-B: In automatic presentation, a sustained passage with no chart
  references returns to the atelier; approaching the next calculated reference
  brings the chart forward. Follow spoken offsets, including streamed speech,
  replay and seeking. Preserve manual pin, grouped aspects, dated sky identity,
  reduced motion and movable inset. Use a short lookahead and bounded grace to
  avoid flicker, never a permanent last-target camera lock.
- ED03 / ED-C: Every generation route displays incoming text and feeds complete
  sentences/short clauses into speech immediately. Auto defaults to one phrase
  synthesizing ahead of playback; Gentle remains sequential, both stream.
  Request direct responses without an optional hidden reasoning pass. Preserve
  provider routing, calculation tools, cancellation, muted preference, account
  ownership, interrupted-text recovery and completed-recording integrity.
  No invented response can conceal upstream latency. Saved audio replay stays.
- ED04 / ED-D: Place a legible small sign beside the actual cookie plate reading
  FREE HALLOWEEN COOKIES in Horrorween, FREE XMAS COOKIES in the Christmas room,
  and FREE COOKIES in the regular room. Include a short tap invitation and
  seasonal cookie appearance. The sign must not block the individual touch
  targets, drag/inset controls, biting or returning cookies to the plate.
- ED05 / ED-E: Publish source and retained build to GitHub and Surge; record
  actual desktop/mobile-width continuity, timings and limitations separately
  from physical Android and acoustic listening acceptance.

## Continuous demonstration, defined before implementation

Use the signed-in existing account and retain its chart, history and keys. Record
the real UI in a continuous screencast with timestamps and named stills. Do not
erase onboarding/history or fabricate readings to meet a scene.

1. E1 entry: delivered room, existing account. Open language/voice settings,
   select Plain English and Auto/read aloud. Return, ask a neutral question
   about a calculated current-to-natal relation. Observe incoming text before
   completion and speech while text grows; capture submit, first text, first
   playback and final-text times. Judge ED01 in the actual prose: angle, meaning,
   historical rationale, current/natal roles and personal significance, casual
   language. Also inspect an explanation with a different natal endpoint.
2. E2 continues that reading: observe chart -> atelier through a non-chart
   digression -> chart at next reference. Record word positions and view, both
   endpoints/line, and identity. Pin room then chart during playback; restore
   automatic mode by initiating a new reading. Replay/seek saved speech and
   passages. Check reduced motion without losing subject changes.
3. E3 same account: stop streaming voice while text grows; verify no later
   audio restart. Re-enable, exercise opening/selection chat, Insta-now and
   generated-reading modal; each starts progressive text/speech by default.
   Modal stop/close and sharing must not overlap/restart speech unexpectedly.
   Observe Gentle and muted behavior, actual provider failure/retry if it
   occurs, and preserve original question. Account change invalidates work;
   private sign-in transition and unobserved provider variants stay explicit.
4. E4 return to atelier: inspect Halloween sign, select one cookie, bite, return,
   drag another, keyboard-select the third. Switch Christmas and regular room,
   inspect matching sign/design; restore Horrorween. At phone width repeat tap,
   bite and inset movement with legible sign and reachable composer. Restore
   desktop, refresh, and confirm saved reading/style/theme continuity. Real
   Android touch and listening require the actual device, not viewport inference.
5. E5 final delivery: verify named Surge release/source identity, live loaded
   assets and retained recordings. Map ED01–05 to observed times and explicitly
   retain failed/missing scenes. Whole-product obligations are not closed here.

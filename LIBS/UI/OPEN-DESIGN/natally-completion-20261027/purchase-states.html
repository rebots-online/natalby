Purchase presentations

Every state Natally shows between a purchase button and her first paid answer. Each frame uses the approved paywall's dialog geometry and artwork, so the purchase feels like one continuous surface. Frames marked paywall are the original paywall driven by its own API; frames marked host panel replace the paywall iframe inside the same dialog.

Dotted values are runtime bindings, shown with tester policy v3 sample values.Design artifact only: no payment, grant or purchase has occurred.
